# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-20

First release.

- Full-screen sleeve poster replacing Spotify's cinema view: fitted gothic title, duotone cover,
  record that slides out and spins at 33⅓ rpm, entrance and track-change choreography.
- 3D turntable in the Now Playing panel: inertial platter, tonearm that follows the track,
  tempo / key / time signature from Spotify's audio analysis, `BST-84xxx` catalog numbers.
- Analog VU meters driven by per-segment loudness.
- Record-sleeve cards, duotone artist photos, numbered catalog sections, page-change sweep.
- Live wallpaper colors through matugen, with a top-bar toggle for cover-art ink.
- `install.sh` with `--matugen`, `--link`, `--no-restart` and `--uninstall`.
